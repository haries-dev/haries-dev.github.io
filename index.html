Research

Published Research

Springer · ICDEC 2023 & Springer International 2025
USB-Sentinel: A Generalized AIO Software-Assisted Insider Threat Detection Framework
Published Feb 27, 2025  ·  Haries Palaniappan et al.  ·  DOI: 10.1007/978-981-97-8476-9_29
USB-Sentinel is an All-In-One software-assisted insider threat detection framework to monitor, analyze, and mitigate unauthorized USB device activities within enterprise environments. Provides real-time behavioral analysis focusing on data exfiltration, unauthorized device usage, and malware injection risks.
Dynamic USB event monitoring with real-time anomaly detection
ML-based behavioral threat analysis
Device whitelisting / blacklisting policy enforcement
Malware detection via sandboxing & signature scanning
Enterprise-ready centralized logging and alerting
Prevents BadUSB, rogue devices & keylogger attacks
